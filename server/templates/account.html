Trade settings

{{ m.csrf_field(csrf) }}

Tap a section to open it. Each one shows the main choices; More options has the rest. Changes apply to the next signal, trades already open keep their stop and targets.

What do these words mean?
Lot
How big a trade is. 0.01 lots is the smallest: on gold it makes or loses about $1 every time the price moves $1.
Pip
A small step in the gold price: 1 pip = $0.10 of price, so 50 pips = a $5 move.
SL (stop loss)
The price where a losing trade closes, so it can't lose more.
TP (take profit)
The price where a winning trade closes and keeps the profit. Signals often give TP1, TP2 and TP3.
Breakeven
Moving the stop loss to the price you got in at, so the trade can't lose any more.
Layers
Splitting one signal into a few smaller orders across the group's price range.
Equity
Your balance plus or minus what your open trades are making right now.
